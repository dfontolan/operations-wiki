Copy this structure when creating a new operational procedure.

## Purpose

State what this procedure achieves and when to use it.

## Prerequisites

- Required access or tools
- Information to have ready

## Procedure

1. Describe the first action.
2. Describe the next action.
3. Confirm the expected result.

## Troubleshooting

Describe common problems, their likely causes, and what to do next.

## Ownership and review

- **Owner:** Team or role responsible
- **Review cycle:** For example, every six months
- **Last reviewed:** YYYY-MM-DD
