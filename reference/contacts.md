Maintain the people, teams, and escalation paths required to run services safely.

| Situation | First contact | Escalate to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service disruption | On-call responder | Incident lead |
| Security concern | Security team | Security lead |
| Vendor issue | Service owner | Vendor manager |

Do not place personal phone numbers or other sensitive contact details in a public repository.
