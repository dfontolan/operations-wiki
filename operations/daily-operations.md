## Purpose

Use this checklist to record the team's regular operating tasks.

## Checklist

1. Review alerts, queues, and overnight handover notes.
2. Check the health of critical services.
3. Review scheduled work due today.
4. Record anything needing follow-up in the team's tracking system.

## Handover

Include completed work, outstanding risks, and the next action for each open item.
