## First actions

1. Confirm the impact and start an incident record.
2. Notify the incident lead and relevant responders.
3. Stabilise the service before investigating the root cause.

## Communication

Provide concise updates that state the impact, current action, and next update time. Avoid speculation.

## Close-out

Record the resolution, customer impact, follow-up actions, and lessons learned.
